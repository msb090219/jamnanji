# NCEA Level 3 Mechanics coverage audit

Jamnanji's playable theory bank is aligned to **AS91524: Demonstrate understanding of mechanical systems**. The 2023 version of the standard remains the basis of the 2026 external assessment specification.

## Assessment approach

The game does not use numerical-substitution exercises. It rehearses the written reasoning that supports NCEA responses:

- identifying the relevant physical principle and its direction or conditions;
- explaining why a relationship applies in the given system;
- linking equations symbolically to establish a result;
- interpreting force, motion, energy, graph, and phase relationships;
- comparing two cases while holding the correct variables constant;
- connecting several principles into an Excellence-level justification.

The three internal question levels mean:

| Game level | Revision purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Achievement: recognise and apply one relevant relationship with correct physical meaning |
| Level 2 | Merit: explain a relationship or connect two steps |
| Level 3 | Excellence: connect several concepts or justify/derive a conclusion |

## Standard coverage

### Translational motion

AS91524 scope: centre of mass in one and two dimensions; conservation of momentum and impulse in two dimensions.

Covered strands:

- one-dimensional centre of mass and balancing moments;
- independent mass-weighted centre-of-mass coordinates in two dimensions;
- motion of a system centre of mass under external forces;
- system boundaries and internal/external force distinction;
- vector momentum and perpendicular components;
- vector impulse, direction changes, and force–time graph area;
- isolated elastic/inelastic collision reasoning;
- collision time, fixed momentum change, and average force.

### Circular motion and gravity

AS91524 scope: velocity, acceleration, and resultant force for circular motion involving two or more forces; Newton's law of gravitation; satellite motion.

Covered strands:

- centripetal force as an inward resultant rather than an additional force;
- vertical-circle force equations at different positions;
- apparent weight on curved paths;
- frictionless banked-turn derivation;
- conical-pendulum force components;
- inverse-square gravitation;
- circular-orbit speed derivation;
- orbital period and `T² ∝ r³` derivation;
- satellite-mass cancellation;
- gravitational weight versus apparent weight in orbit.

### Rotating systems

AS91524 scope: constant-angular-acceleration motion; torque; rotational inertia; conservation of angular momentum; conservation of energy.

Covered strands:

- angular/linear kinematic analogies;
- `d = rθ`, `v = rω`, and `a = rα` reasoning;
- perpendicular force components and torque;
- signed net torque and angular acceleration;
- mass distribution and rotational inertia;
- conservation and direction of angular momentum;
- objects joining rotating systems;
- changes in inertia and angular speed;
- work done while redistributing mass;
- rotational work–energy relationships.

### Oscillating systems

AS91524 scope: SHM conditions; angular frequency; displacement, velocity, and acceleration with time; phasor diagrams and reference circles; damping, driving, resonance, and conservation of energy.

Covered strands:

- the defining condition `a = −ω²y`;
- phase relationships among displacement, velocity, and acceleration;
- reference-circle and phasor interpretations;
- symbolic differentiation of SHM equations;
- kinetic/elastic energy exchange and conservation;
- amplitude-squared energy dependence and amplitude-independent ideal period;
- damping as mechanical-energy transfer;
- critical damping;
- energy input and phase during driven resonance;
- changes to the resonance curve as damping increases.

## Draw rules

- The playable pool contains only questions explicitly marked as written theory.
- Legacy questions are admitted through a reviewed ID allowlist after an individual scope, depth, and no-calculation check; unlisted legacy material cannot be drawn.
- Act topic filters keep each stage focused on its relevant mechanical system.
- Within an eligible topic and difficulty pool, questions with similar correct/distractor lengths are drawn before strongly length-biased items, with random order inside each balance tier.
- A question ID is recorded when drawn and cannot appear again in the same run.
- Answer positions are shuffled independently from question selection.

Current playable depth: 39 approved questions in Act I, 30 in Act II, and 66 in Act III. Act III has 11 map floors, compared with eight in each earlier act, and its larger pool supports the additional encounters without recycling questions.
